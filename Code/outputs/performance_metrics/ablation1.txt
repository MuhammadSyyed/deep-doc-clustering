Ablation Discussion (draft)

An ablation study was conducted to assess the contribution of the autoencoder and the
proposed semantic refinement module within SDCN under SBERT embeddings. Results indicate
that no single configuration is uniformly superior across datasets. 

1. The full SDCN model achieves the strongest performance on BBCNews and AGNews, suggesting that the joint AE–GCN
objective is effective when document categories are relatively well separated.

2. In contrast, removing the autoencoder (`SDCN-AE`) yields clear gains on Reuters and especially 20Newsgroups,
where the full model performs poorly. This implies that AE reconstruction can conflict with discriminative
clustering on noisier or finer-grained corpora, and that the graph–clustering pathway alone may be more
robust in such settings.

3. Semantic refinement (`SDCN+SemRefine`) improves all reported metrics on DBpedia,
indicating that injecting original SBERT semantics into the final GCN representation is beneficial for
broader entity/topic categories; however, the same module is neutral to detrimental on Reuters, 20Newsgroups, 
and AGNews.

Overall, the ablation shows that architectural components should be matched to dataset characteristics
rather than applied uniformly: full SDCN is a strong default for cleaner collections, AE ablation helps under highe
topic ambiguity, and semantic refinement provides dataset-specific gains rather than a consistent uplift.

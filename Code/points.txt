This suggests:

IDEC may help when:

* classes are globally separable
* manifold structure is rigid
* latent refinement sharpens category boundaries

and hurt when:

* semantic neighborhoods are already finely balanced
* latent compression destroys subtle distinctions

The effectiveness of IDEC depends strongly on the embedding space and dataset semantic structure. While IDEC substantially improves clustering in sparse lexical spaces and rigid semantic datasets, it may distort already well-structured semantic manifolds produced by pretrained language models.
